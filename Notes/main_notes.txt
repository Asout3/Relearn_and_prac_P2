When testing an unauthorized call that should revert, you must assert that all relevant state remains unchanged: role assignments, balances, allowances, total supply, ownership, paused status, and any other contract-specific variables. You should also assert the correct revert reason and that no events were emitted. This proves the contract did not partially execute before reverting.
